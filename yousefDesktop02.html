My Military Experience

One of the biggest chapters of my life was joining the United States Navy. What started as a career became a life-changing experience that helped me grow personally and professionally. During my 20 years of service, I had the opportunity to lead, mentor, and work with people from different backgrounds. I eventually reached the rank of Master Chief Petty Officer, something I am extremely proud of. The Navy taught me discipline, accountability, leadership, teamwork, and the importance of helping others succeed. Those lessons continue to guide me today.
