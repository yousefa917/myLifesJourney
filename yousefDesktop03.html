My Lifestyle, Family, and Values

Making it to Retirement

My lifestyle reflects a combination of my Yemeni upbringing, my experiences growing up in Brooklyn, and everything I learned throughout my military career.

Family, respect, loyalty, and hard work are very important to me. I believe in enjoying life, traveling, experiencing different cultures, spending time with family, and continuing to improve myself.

I also believe in giving back. Working with high school students allows me to share my experiences and help prepare the next generation for their future. Seeing young people grow, develop confidence, and become leaders is one of the most rewarding parts of my life.
